# About Ashen

I'm **Ashen**, a vibe coder exploring what I can make with AI-assisted development.

Hulk in Manhattan is a fan homage to *The Incredible Hulk: Ultimate Destruction*. It is also an experiment: how far can a team of AI agents get in Unreal Engine 5 when one person picks the direction, plays every build, and says what looks or feels wrong? It grew out of my Spider-Man 2 port of Shikhar's Spiderbench. The same Manhattan, with a very different hero.

The repository records the implementation, the asset sources, and the mistakes along the way, including the clips that lost blind A/B tests. AI assistance is part of the process; it is not a substitute for a game that is fun to play.
