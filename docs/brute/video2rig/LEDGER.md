### Fit pass 2026-10-29 (Opus 5.5): the lock_feet floor bug, toe_min finding

- **Root cause of the broken legs in every ground fit so far**: video2rig `reader/fit.py` `lock_feet` set the pinned foot's target height
  to `rest` (measured from the floor) without adding the floor (`tgt @ up` is from the origin). The Brute's floor is -0.61 m (armature
  space, creature height 2.34), so every pinned foot was sent 0.61 m up and the leg folded to its joint limits trying to reach it
  ("farthest a pinned foot stayed from its place" 12-14% of the height). That is where the "LeftFoot/RightFoot at the end of their range"
  and "Leg turns 150 degrees in one frame" warnings came from. Idle frame 0: leg keypoints 30-90 px off the reading with the bug,
  0.3-2.7 px after the fix (the start pose itself reads 1-2 px). Fix: fork branch `brute-lockfeet-floor` (c60a645 + ede6cce, worktree
  `~/video2rig-lockfix`, pushed; mac-mps untouched), plus a road.py change so `video2rig run --set KEY=VALUE` reaches the reader fit.
- **Affected, still on the buggy fit (refit with `--fork-dir ~/video2rig-lockfix` before any A/B)**: Idle 14.0%, Clap 13.7%, Land 11.9%,
  Lift 13.1%, PunchL 13.5%, PunchR v2 13.7%, Slap 12.6%, Throw 14.3%, Uppercut 12.0% (pinned-foot miss in their run.log). Walk, Run,
  WallClimb are refit (rows above).
- **toe_min 356-422 mm on Idle / Slap / Lift is not a refine_brute.py metric bug**: toe_min is the lowest vertex whose dominant weight is
  a ToeBase bone; the fitted feet really were pitched toes-up and the legs folded (Blender renders of `Idle_v2r_v1_refined.blend`: frame
  1 standing on heels, frame 90 the left foot lifted to the hand; the video2rig review film shows the same). The real toe minimum of those
  clips is unknown until they are refit with the fix; their current GLBs carry the broken legs.
- **refine_brute.py additions**: `--treadmill` (in-place Walk/Run: plants pinned in a ground frame sliding back at the take's median
  planted-toe velocity, drift measured in that frame) and `--window A B` (cut a steady cycle before trim/loop).
